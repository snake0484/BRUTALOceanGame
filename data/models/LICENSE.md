Model brushes pick from this list (`models.json`) only: game assets, extended by game data or mods, never player files.
A model is JSON: positions in 1/1024 m, +Y out of the hull, +Z along the stroke, origin on the surface, polygon faces
(3-16 corners) wound counter-clockwise seen from outside.
